FAQ

Answers before you book.

Common questions about classes, language, scheduling, trial lessons, and advanced study.

Who can study at the academy?

The academy serves women and children at beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels. Boys may be accepted based on age and suitability.

Are lessons available in English and Arabic?

Yes. The site is English-first for international families, and lessons can be guided in English or Arabic according to the student's need.

What happens in the free trial?

Sara listens to the student's recitation, identifies strengths and correction priorities, and recommends a practical course path.

Can children join if they are complete beginners?

Yes. Beginner children can start with Arabic letters, short surahs, listening, repetition, and gentle reading practice.

Is Ijazah available for every student?

Ijazah preparation is for advanced students after assessment. Some students may first need Tajweed stability, fluency, or memorization strengthening.

How are schedules arranged?

Scheduling depends on time zone, availability, student age, and lesson frequency. Share preferred times in the booking form.
